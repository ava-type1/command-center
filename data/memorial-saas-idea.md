# Memorial Pages SaaS — Business Idea

**Date:** February 9, 2026  
**Origin:** Built grandparents' (Jerry & Gloria Rhodes) memorial page and realized the process could be productized.

---

## The Pitch

A SaaS platform that lets funeral homes offer beautiful, living memorial websites to families. Not a static obituary — a growing, collaborative tribute that family members can visit and add to forever.

## The Problem

- Obituaries are static, printed once, forgotten
- Families have no central place to collect memories, photos, stories
- Funeral homes want digital upsells but don't have tech capability
- Existing memorial sites (Legacy.com, EverLoved) are generic templates with no soul
- Nobody is doing historical newspaper research to discover the deceased's life story

## The Differentiator

**AI-powered memorial creation** — what took a professional web designer a week, we do in 30 minutes:
- Auto-search historical newspaper archives (UFDC, newspapers.com, etc.) to find real articles about the person
- AI-written narrative that weaves the life story together
- Beautiful, custom-feeling design from templates
- Historical discoveries families didn't even know about ("Your grandpa was on the city council?!")

## How It Works

### For Funeral Homes (B2B)
1. Sign up for monthly subscription
2. Enter deceased's basic info (name, dates, family, locations)
3. Platform auto-generates a memorial page with newspaper research
4. Funeral home reviews, customizes, delivers to family
5. Family gets a link + optional QR code

### For Families (End Users)
1. Receive memorial link from funeral home
2. Share with family via text/Facebook/email
3. Family members can add stories, photos, videos
4. Page grows over time — becomes a living tribute
5. QR code available for headstone/urn/frame

## Revenue Model (Final)

### Funeral Home Pays Us
- **$50 wholesale per page** (includes first year hosting)
- No monthly subscription — per-page only
- Zero risk for the funeral home, they only pay when they sell

### Funeral Home Charges Family
- Whatever they want — suggested $149-199
- They keep the difference ($99-149 profit per page)
- One simple product: "memorial page" — no tiers to explain

### Family Pays Us Directly (via the page itself)
- After first year included, family sees hosting options on the page:
- **$29/year** — annual renewal
- **$249 one-time** — page hosted forever
- Offered on the live page, not by the funeral director
- Most families will choose lifetime while the page is fresh and being actively shared
- QR code included free (digital file, family takes to monument maker)

### Why This Pricing Works
- Funeral home has zero friction — sell page, get paid, done
- No upselling grieving families — the product sells itself
- Lifetime fee captured early while emotional connection is strong
- $50/page is 95%+ margin for us
- $249 lifetime is 99%+ margin (hosting costs ~$1/year forever)

## Unit Economics

**Per page breakdown:**
- Our revenue: $50 (wholesale) + $249 (lifetime, ~70% of families) = $299
- Our cost: ~$2 to create, ~$1/year to host
- **Margin: 99%**

**Conservative (5 funeral homes, 4 pages/month each):**
- 240 pages/year
- Wholesale: $12,000
- Lifetime fees (70%): 168 × $249 = $41,832
- Annual fees (30%): compound over time
- Costs: ~$1,800/year
- **Year 1 profit: ~$52,000**

**Moderate (20 funeral homes, 4 pages/month):**
- 960 pages/year
- **Year 1 profit: ~$212,000**

**Optimistic (50 funeral homes, 5 pages/month):**
- 3,000 pages/year
- **Year 1 profit: ~$667,000**

## Market

- ~19,000 funeral homes in the US
- Industry does ~2.5 million funerals/year
- Average funeral cost: $7,000-12,000 (memorial page is a tiny add-on)
- Industry is slow to adopt tech — huge opportunity for first mover
- Consolidation happening (SCI/Dignity Memorial buying small homes) — both indie and chain opportunities
